SelfHelp Registry

The official catalog of SelfHelp platform releases (backend, frontend, worker, scheduler Docker images consumed by the SelfHelp Manager) and plugins (advertised to SelfHelp hosts). Everything resolves from registry.json; each version links a signed release document.
